## Grade A prerequisites

Before ordinary official UI input, fsync a unique intent ID, host-monotonic time,
current first-observed world sequence, document/match/player/client identity,
complete source and target snapshots, intended typed vector, UI action type,
all visible force identities, supply-line/relay facts and outstanding intents.
Pair a separate UI-delivery record by intent ID and identical player/epoch.
Mouse delivery alone never establishes server acceptance.

Use a before-only deterministic no-action source baseline for the application
interval. Exact depletion of each unit category must equal the newborn vector,
after known production/decay adjustments. Unknown production, inbound arrival,
upgrade, overflow, automatic deployment, hidden source or incomplete inventory
refuses causal attribution. Retain source-before, predicted-no-action, observed
source-after and residual vector. Never choose a newborn by matching quantity;
select uniquely by reliable new lineage, owner and intended source/target first,
then compare composition and conservation.

Target, composition, epoch continuity and the preregistered sequence window must
all agree. Exactly one intent-to-effect assignment must exist across the entire
window and all outstanding intents. Duplicate or same-tick same-pair actions,
multiple compatible newborns, observer-ID reuse and missing samples are
AMBIGUOUS/UNRESOLVED, never false unique. A known supply line or possible automatic
birth affecting the source/window prevents Grade A unless independently excluded
by a predeclared control; unknown is not absence. Quantity equality alone fails.

## Pilot and frozen formal window

Run a separate bounded pilot of at most five eligible single ordinary actions in
a future phase. Keep all intents/failures/no-birth outcomes; inspect the observed
distribution of first eligible effect offsets from the durable intent tick.
Do not use it as formal clean/adversarial evidence. Before any formal dispatch,
freeze one inclusive world-tick offset interval, its missing-sample policy, fixed
end-of-window timeout, allowed host lease limits and ambiguity handling in a
versioned cohort manifest. If pilot offsets/certainty cannot define a finite
usable interval, stop as PARTIAL. No hard-coded 0–1 tick claim is assumed.
A later changed window requires a new preregistration and separate cohort;
previous failures remain in the original denominator.

## Formal cohort allocation and truth labels

Target exactly 40 planned trials, at most one new attempt per listed trial:
15 clean ordinary dispatches; 15 adversarial/ambiguous trials; 10 variation trials.
Freeze the ordered trial manifest after pilot and before outcomes. Clean cases
vary ordinary amount, adjacent path length and allowed target ownership.
Adversarial strata cover duplicate intent, same-tick compatible actions,
preexisting compatible forces, line/automatic-event interference, and independently
established UI rejection/no-send controls. Do not force an illegal or unsafe UI
operation to fill a stratum. Unavailable strata are NOT_RUN and do not become
successes or get silently replaced. The last ten cover amount/target/continuous
operation variation. All attempts consume slots, including UI failure and timeout.

False-unique truth must be independently adjudicated from complete retained
before/action/after records and predeclared controls; the association tool's own
label is not ground truth. Unresolved truth is UNKNOWN and excluded only from
truth-conditioned error-rate calculations, with counts explicitly reported.
It remains in total attempts and prevents claiming full adversarial coverage.
Review every unique claim, plus all ambiguous/rejected outcomes. Freeze truth
rules before trials and preserve any disagreements and counterexamples.

## Acceptance gates

Primary: FALSE_UNIQUE = 0 among all independently adjudicated trials. One false
unique fails the cohort and prevents Grade A promotions pending correction.
Clean: at least 14/15 independently eligible clean trials correctly unique, with
zero false unique. Report planned, attempted, evaluable, unique, ambiguous,
unresolved, failed UI and NOT_RUN counts separately. Partial cohort is PARTIAL,
not PASS. A supported positive attribution may be Grade A only after the above
source-conservation and alternative-cause gates pass independently.

For independently rejected/no-send controls, the expected label is
NO_ATTRIBUTABLE_EFFECT_WITHIN_FIXED_WINDOW only if complete observations show no
compatible world effect and no source-depletion residual. A failed mouse call or
lack of newborn alone is not proof of rejection/no effect. Partial mouse delivery,
background changes, missing ticks or uncertain client acceptance are UNKNOWN or
AMBIGUOUS. Any compatible effect in a verified rejected trial is investigated
and must not be silently attributed or called harmless. This is a bounded visible
outcome claim, not proof that no delayed server action can ever occur.

Report causal uniqueness and decision metrics alongside exact-state comparisons;
no pilot/formal ledger success proves full combat/arrival semantics or M2 PASS.
Callback/queue research remains STOPPED; only a concrete false unique, systematic
ledger failure, or changed client operation path justifies a separately bounded
hypothesis. Ordinary Grade A event and 5–10s trajectory targets remain unfulfilled.
